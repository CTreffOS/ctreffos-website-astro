# About

Welcome to Chaostreff Osnabrück!

People of all genders, backgrounds and experience levels meet here to work on projects and learn from each other.
You can try things out and ask questions, whether you've been tinkering for years or are just curious.

Our interests include robotics, open-source projects, IT security and digital art.
You can bring your own ideas or see what others are working on.

We also organise workshops, talks and hackathons.
We value treating each other with respect, listening and taking different experiences seriously.

Come along to meet us. You're welcome to visit once or join us regularly.
