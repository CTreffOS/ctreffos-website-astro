# Hackspace

Welcome to the **Rabbithole** of the Chaostreff Osnabrück!<br/>
We're located at the [Uni AStA Osnabrück](/en/hackspace#directions).

At the Rabbithole, we work on projects and try out technology together. You don't need any experience to join in.

We have a lot of tools and materials on site, here just an overview:

- 3D Printer
- Soldering station with hot air station
- Mobile soldering irons
- Laptops
- [senseBoxes](https://sensebox.de/)
- Cold beverages including Mate

## Directions

Our Hackspace Rabbithole is located in the Osnabrücker city center in the [AStA of the University Osnabrück, Alte Münze 12, 49074 Osnabrück](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/696172783).

The entrance is in the passageway. If you have problems finding us, please call our [phone](/en/contact) in the Hackspace.

### By public transportation

The hackspace is located near [Neumarkt](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/1747348346), the central hub of Osnabrücks bus network.

The VOS provides [a departure board](https://www.vos.info/fahrplaner/#depart!Z%7COsnabr%C3%BCck%20Neumarkt) on their website.

If you arrive by train, get off at Osnabrück Hbf (Osnabrück Central Train Station). To get to the hackspace/Neumarkt from there, you can take the next bus from bus platform 1. Alternatively you can walk to the Hackspace in 15 minutes.

### By bike

There is a [bike stand](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/473015925) directly in front of the house. Additional bike parking options can be found on the backside of the [library Alte Münze](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/1747348346).

### By car

Due to the central location, there is unfortunately no free parking available within the inner city. However, there are several paid parking options in the surrounding area.

Right across from the Hackspace you can find the parking garage Ledenhof Garage (Exit Zentrum).
Information about occupancy and prices can be found on the [OPG website](https://www.parken-osnabrueck.de/parken/parkplatzsuche/detail/parkhaus-ledenhof-garage.html).
During large events taking place in the [OsnabrückHalle](https://www.osnabrueckhalle.de/besuchen/programm/) or in the inner city (e.g. Maiwoche), it might be full.

### Accessibility

Our rooms are two floors above ground level. There is no lift, and some passageways are narrow. The rooms are therefore not wheelchair accessible. We sometimes meet outside, especially in summer. [Email us](/en/contact) so we can discuss whether and how we can arrange a meeting that works for you.

If you need help to find the Hackspace, or for questions, please contact us. During the meetings, you can also reach us via our [hackspace phone](/en/contact).
