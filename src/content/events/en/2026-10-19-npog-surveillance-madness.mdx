Surveillance is intended to create control. But surveillance can serve many different interests, and we cannot possibly keep track of all of them. Devices used for surveillance are installed all around us, willingly carried with us, or secretly implanted. Yet most of us have little technical understanding of what these devices actually do.

What is going on behind the apparent opacity? What is being measured? What can be done with measurement data? How does surveillance work? What the hell is Palantir, anyway? This talk is meant to be a short excursion through the technology behind it all, offering answers that raise even more questions.

**The talk will be held in German.**
