On Saturday, 3rd October, Osnabrück celebrates volunteer work with the community festival
["Engagierte Stadt"](https://erleben.osnabrueck.de/de/stadt-erleben/city-events/buergerfest/) at Ledenhof.
Clubs, initiatives and organisations showcase their work with a booth or on stage.

Of course, we're also there: From 10:00 to 20:00, we invite you to get to know us, learn soldering and take home your own PCB!
No prior knowledge required. We provide everything you need.

Our tent is on the street Neuer Graben (in front of Osnabrück Castle) to the right of the stage. Come by!
